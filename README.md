# Fast Interview Prep Skill

> A reusable AI Skill for fast, personalized interview preparation based on JD, resume, company context, and real interview experiences.

## Overview

Fast Interview Prep Skill is a reusable AI Skill designed to help candidates prepare for interviews more efficiently.

It combines:

- Job Description (JD)
- Resume
- Company and business context
- Real interview experiences

to generate a structured and personalized interview preparation notebook.

## Core Design Principle

> **Interview experiences determine what is actually asked.  
> JD determines what should be prioritized.  
> Resume determines where to conduct deep dives.  
> Company context determines how to construct business scenarios.**

## Input

The Skill uses:

- Resume
- Job Description
- Company name
- Optional company introduction
- Public interview experiences

## Output

The final interview preparation notebook includes:

1. Core Competitiveness
2. Self Introduction
3. Resume Deep Dive
4. Professional Questions
5. Company Understanding
6. HR Questions
7. Questions to Ask

## Workflow

```text
JD + Resume + Company Context
              ↓
        Role Identification
              ↓
   Professional Capability Mapping
              ↓
    Interview Experience Research
              ↓
        Resume Deep Dive
              ↓
     Professional Questions
              ↓
       Company Understanding
              ↓
          HR Questions
              ↓
        Questions to Ask
              ↓
   Final Interview Preparation
